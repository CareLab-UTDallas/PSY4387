Evidence Appraisal: Biological Claims

Knowing a mechanism is one skill. Judging whether the evidence actually supports a claim about it is a harder one, but that is the skill that distinguishes a consumer of research from a scientist. This activity gives you a small appraisal toolkit, then puts it to work on studies that make biological claims about how therapy works. The studies here are constructed for practice, but the reasoning is exactly what you will use on real ones.
