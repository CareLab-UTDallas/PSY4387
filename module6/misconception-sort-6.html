Reductionism Traps: Sort the Brain Claims

Brain claims carry a persuasive glow, which is why they need a few extra defenses. For each statement, decide whether it’s a reductionism trap (a common misreading) or an accurate claim, then check your reasoning.

The balanced stance this module is building is to take the biology seriously, since it is real and ignoring it leaves an incomplete picture, without letting it intimidate you into fatalism or dazzle you with glowing images. Biology and psychology are two descriptions of one thing.
