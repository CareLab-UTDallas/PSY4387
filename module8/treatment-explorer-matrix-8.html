Treatment Explorer — Mechanism Matrix

Evidence-based interventions, organized by the mechanism they target. Open each of the five treatments to read what it does, then build the comparison matrix below. The goal is not to memorize five therapy names, it is to see that treatments cluster by the process they change, so two very different-looking approaches can be mechanistic cousins and two similar-sounding ones can be opposites.

This module's task. Open each of the five cards and read its four fields. Then complete the matrix, choosing the correct value for every cell, and answer the two comparison questions: which two treatments are most mechanistically similar, and which two are most different. You're done when the matrix is complete and both comparisons are answered.
