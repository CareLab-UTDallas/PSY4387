Cognitive Work: Sort the Claims

Cognitive ideas have leaked into popular self-help, so they are widely misunderstood. For each statement, decide whether it’s a misconception (a common misreading of cognitive work) or an accurate claim, then check your reasoning.

Careful cognitive work is not the popular slogan version. The goal is accuracy rather than forced positivity, feelings are examined rather than scolded, and the techniques only work inside a trusting relationship. Watch for the places where the self-help version quietly gets it wrong.
