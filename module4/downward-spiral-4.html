Two Kinds of Downward: The Loop and the Arrow

Two ideas in this module both move “downward,” and they are easy to mix up. The loop is a self-perpetuating cycle that worsens over time: a belief shapes attention, attention finds “evidence,” and the evidence feeds the belief. The downward arrow is a way of tracing a single thought down through its layers to the core belief underneath. Use the toggle to work with each one. They are different tools for different jobs.
