Build a Thought Record

A thought record is the core tool of cognitive restructuring, a simple worksheet that turns a swirling, believable thought into an object you can examine. You'll complete several rows using dropdowns, then build one or two of your own. The steps stay the same each time: catch the thought, name the feeling and what it drives, spot the distortion, and write a fairer, more accurate thought.

Nothing here is recorded. Everything you select or type stays in your browser on your own screen. Nothing is saved, sent, or transmitted anywhere, and no one, including your instructor, can see what you write. When you close or refresh the page, it's gone.
