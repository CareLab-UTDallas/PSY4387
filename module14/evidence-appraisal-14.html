Evidence Appraisal: Transdiagnostic and Diagnosis-Specific Claims

Judging whether the evidence supports a claim about a treatment requires different skills from knowing the treatment. The six appraisal questions below apply to four constructed claims about transdiagnostic and diagnosis-specific treatment. Each claim has two questions, one on the best reading and one on the check that would most change the conclusion. The studies and every number are constructed for practice, and the reasoning carries over to real ones.
