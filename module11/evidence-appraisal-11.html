Evidence Appraisal: Four Treatment Claims

Judging whether a trial supports a claim takes different skills from knowing the research terms. The seven questions below apply to four constructed claims about treatments. Each claim has several real limits, and the task is to find the one that bears most directly on what the claim says. The studies and numbers are invented for practice.
