Work the arithmetic one step at a time, then test your judgment on seven invented cases.

Invented practice values. Every score, standard deviation, reliability, cutpoint, name, and clinic on this page is invented for practice. Real instruments have published standard deviations, reliabilities, and cutpoints that differ by sample and population, and conventions vary on which reliability coefficient to use, how a cutpoint is set, and whether to use 1.96. Look those values up for any real instrument before using them. This page is for learning and does not support decisions about any real person. Nothing you type is saved or sent.
