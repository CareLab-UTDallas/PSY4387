Digital Care Claims: Sort the Claims

Claims about digital care, access, and implementation often sound more settled than the evidence is. For each statement, decide whether it is a common misconception or an accurate claim, then check your reasoning against the module. Any figures in the explanations that come from published studies are named by source, and figures marked invented are for practice.

Some of these claims are accurate and some are common misconceptions. For each one, ask what comparison, count, or design would be needed to support it.
