The Regulation Chain

An emotion is not a single event that fires or does not. It unfolds as a short story in a sequence, and there are five points along that sequence where a person can step in and change how it ends. Pick a point in one person's chain, choose a strategy there, and watch how it plays out. The model is a map, not a verdict, so most points have a version that helps and a version that quietly backfires.
