The Present and the Story That Made It

Two questions circle each other in every real case. The emotional question is what this person does with their feelings, moment to moment. The developmental question is how they came to handle feelings this way. For each case, name the present-moment regulation strategy and the developmental origin that shaped it, then see how a plan works at both levels at once, using the therapy relationship itself as part of the treatment.

Present-moment emotion and lifelong development are two views of one continuous story. A strategy that looks puzzling in the present often makes complete sense as a childhood adaptation that has outlived the situation it was built for.
