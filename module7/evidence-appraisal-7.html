Evidence Appraisal: Developmental Claims

Knowing a mechanism is one skill. Judging whether the evidence actually supports a claim about it is a harder one, but that is the skill that distinguishes a consumer of research from a scientist. This activity gives you a small appraisal toolkit, then puts it to work on studies that make developmental and emotional claims about who struggles and why. The studies here are constructed for practice, but the reasoning is exactly what you will use on real ones.
