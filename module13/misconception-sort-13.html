Common Misconceptions: Sort the Claims

Claims about ethics, law, and confidentiality often sound confident and settled. For each statement, decide whether it is a common misconception or an accurate claim, then check your reasoning against the module. Laws differ by place, so ask what the clinician would have to check where they work.

Some of these claims are accurate and some are common misconceptions. For each one, ask which source of authority would settle it and what the clinician would have to confirm.
