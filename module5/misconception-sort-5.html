Interpersonal Claims: Sort the Statements

The interpersonal level has a few slippery ideas, and one term (negative reinforcement) may be the most misunderstood in all of psychology. For each statement, decide whether it’s a misconception or an accurate claim, then check your reasoning.

The stance this module builds is to widen the lens from the person to the pattern, without turning that into blame. Loops are mutual, relief is a powerful teacher, and change can start with one person altering their half. Watch for the places the straight-line, blame-first view quietly gets it wrong.
