The Loop That Builds Itself

Relationships run in circles, not straight lines. Click each step of this loop to see how two people, each making a reasonable choice, build a trap neither of them intended. Then find where the loop can be interrupted.

The situation. Theo feels intensely anxious in social situations. His partner, Nadia, has learned to smooth things over for him out of love. When they are invited to a party, Nadia makes the excuses. Neither of them can see the loop, because from the inside it just feels like caring for each other.
