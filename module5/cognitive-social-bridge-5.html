Where the Cognitive Meets the Social

In Module 4 you built the thought record and learned to spot distortions inside one person's head. This module widens the lens: those same cognitive patterns are often maintained, and even created, by the loops between people. For each case, name the distortion in the person's head and the mechanism in the loop around them, then see how a mechanism-first clinician picks tools at both levels, choosing by what is keeping the problem going rather than by any brand.

Some of the most powerful change happens at the boundary between the cognitive and the social. The thought record works inside one head; the feedback loop works between people. Skilled practice moves between them, following the person rather than a favorite theory.
