Claim Court: Four Claims on Trial

Judging whether the evidence supports a claim takes different skills from knowing the terms. Seven tools frame the questions to ask. Four claims follow, each with two questions, one on the best reading and one on the check that would most change the conclusion. Claims A, B, and C are constructed for practice and every number in them is invented. Claim D's flyer is constructed and is not a quotation from any paper, and one chip in Claim D summarizes a real published abstract. If anything in these cases affects you personally, you can call or text 988 at any hour, and in an emergency you can call 911.
